About Me

I Speak in Strings

Music isn't just what I do — it's how I think, feel, and communicate. I'm Skill Aurnob, a guitarist and bassist who lives for the moment a riff locks in perfectly with a groove.

Whether I'm laying down a warm bass line that anchors the entire track, or weaving guitar melodies that cut right through the mix, I bring intention and feel to every note. I've played across stages big and small, from intimate club nights to large-scale live events — always chasing that perfect sonic moment.

I also work as a session musician, collaborating with artists, bands, and producers who need a player who listens deeply and delivers. If you have a project that needs real strings and real emotion, let's make something together.
